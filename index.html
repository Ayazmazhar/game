📖 In-Game Instructions & Guide
🎮 1. Game Controls & Movement
• Swipe Gestures: Swipe anywhere on touch screen to turn Snake.
• Touch D-Pad: Use on-screen directional buttons for precise tapping.
• Keyboard: Use Arrow Keys or W A S D to steer.
• 180° Safety Guard: Instant 180° turns are automatically prevented to prevent accidental self-crash.
🕹️ 2. Game Modes
Classic (Endless): Endless survival! Speed increases as score grows.
Time Attack (60s): Race against 60.0s countdown clock!
Level Mode: 42 structured maps with wall barriers & goals.
Ghost Mode: Race against your best run's spectral ghost snake 👻!
🍏 3. Food, Scoring & Power-ups
• Neon Food: Earns +10 points & increases snake length.
• Golden Star Bonus: Appears periodically for 10 seconds! Worth +50 points.
• Speed Scaling: Speed increases gradually (+2% or +5%) as you eat more food.
🎨 4. Customization & Audio
• Select from 5 Snake Skins & 6 Dynamic Themes.
• Choose from 5 Built-in BGM Tracks or upload your own .mp3/.wav file.
• Toggle Sound & Haptic Vibration anytime in settings.
📺 5. Rewarded Ad Revive
• On Game Over, tap "Watch Ad to Revive" to clear surrounding 3x3 cells and continue your run (1x per game session).
